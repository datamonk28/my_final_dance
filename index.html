05 / The last page

And still,
thank you.

Thank you for the gifts, the laughter, the conversations, the comfort and the memories. Thank you for being a part of my life. I don’t want to erase what happened or pretend that it meant nothing. There were beautiful moments, and I will always be grateful for them. Whatever tomorrow holds, I sincerely wish you happiness, peace and everything beautiful life has to offer. This is not an attempt to bring anything back. It is simply my way of saying thank you. Take care. Goodbye.

With gratitude, always.
